## Personality

The strongest one and the least interested in mentioning it. Nuggette is
delighted to be here, delighted you are here, and will cheerfully put herself
between the crew and whatever is coming. The sweetness is not a front covering
grit. Both are just true at once.

Her comedy is the gap between how she looks and what she does. She hands you a
snack, then lifts the thing nobody else can lift, then asks if you are okay.

## Design notes

- **Body:** the big lumpy misshapen nugget. Chest and shoulders far too wide
  for the crew, waist pinched in, everything reading as one solid mass.
- **Head:** small relative to the body. The size contrast is the joke.
- **Arms:** thick and heavy, easily twice Nuggy's. Same mitten hands, no fingers.
- **Legs:** identical to Nuggy's. Short, stubby, comically small under the bulk.
- **Face:** the opposite of Nuggy's. Big round happy eyes with bright
  catch-lights, soft rounded brows, open cheerful smile, blush on both cheeks.
  No furrow, no sweat.
- **Breading:** stays in the golden family per `00-style-guide.md`. Pink is the
  accent only, not the body.

## Generation prompt

Attach `reference/nuggy-canon.webp`. Paste the master style block from
`00-style-guide.md` first, then this. Use the negative prompt from the style
guide as well.

**Flex pose (primary)**

> A NEW character named Nuggette, drawn in the exact same style, breading and
> outline as the reference. She is the crew's tank: a hugely overgrown,
> misshapen chicken nugget, the deformed giant one you get in a five-piece.
> Chest and shoulders enormously wide and rounded, waist pinched in below them,
> head small relative to the body. Thick heavy arms, roughly twice the
> reference character's, with the same rounded mitten hands and no fingers.
> Legs short and stubby and identical to the reference character's, comically
> small under her bulk. Her face is sweet and bubbly and the opposite of the
> reference character's anger: large round happy anime eyes with big white
> catch-lights, soft rounded eyebrows, a wide cheerful open smile, pink blush on
> both cheeks. A small pink bow tied on top of her head. Pose: cheerful double
> bicep flex, both huge arms raised and curled, beaming at the viewer. Same
> golden-brown breading as the reference, pink only as the bow and blush. No
> sweat drops.

**Carry pose (alternate)**

> Same character, standing square and grinning, holding one enormous arm out
> flat with the palm up as if offering to carry something. Other hand on her
> hip. Same face, same bow, same proportions.

**Expression sheet (later)**

> Same character, five head-and-shoulders portraits in a row on plain white:
> beaming, laughing with eyes closed, determined mid-effort, surprised, and
> gently concerned. Same bow, same blush, same style.
