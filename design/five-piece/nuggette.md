## Personality

The strongest one and the least interested in mentioning it. Miss Nuggette is
delighted to be here, delighted you are here, and will cheerfully put herself
between the crew and whatever is coming.

Her comedy is the gap between how she looks and what she does. She hands you a
snack, then lifts the thing nobody else can lift, then asks if you are okay.

The reference art pushes her further than the brief did. She is mid-battle-cry
in it, brows down and one fist up. The build pulls her back towards bubbly where
it can: bright breading on Nuggy's ramp instead of the reference's VHS grade,
bubblegum bows, blush kept. What is left is a great action pose. She still needs
a resting one for the crew shot.

## What the reference says about her

The art is dressed as a 1995 VHS screengrab from an anime called
**燃えろ！食の戦士たち！**, "Burn! Warriors of Food". Its captions give her:

- **黄金の守護神** — golden guardian deity
- **ナゲット・ナディア** — Nugget Nadia, her name in that fiction
- **ポップコーンチキンナゲット** — popcorn chicken nugget, her nugget type
- **必殺技「ハニー・ディップアーマー!!」** — finishing move, "Honey Dip Armour!!"
- **みんなを守るため… アタシは、揚がるわっ!!** — "To protect everyone... I'll
  get fried!!"

Honey Dip Armour is too good to leave in the reference. It is the obvious basis
for her power: she coats herself and takes the hit for the crew.

## Design notes

- **Body:** one big lumpy popcorn mass, far wider than it is tall, reading as a
  single silhouette rather than a torso with parts attached.
- **Head:** not separable from the body. The face sits on the mass.
- **Arms:** thick, one raised in a fist, one low. Rounded mitten hands, no
  fingers.
- **Pigtails:** two lumps off the sides of the head, each tied with a pink bow.
  They are the swingiest thing she has and are rigged as their own groups.
- **Feet:** stubby lumps under the bulk, comically small.
- **Face:** huge round anime eyes with catch-lights, heavy angled brows, open
  shouting mouth with a pink tongue, and hatched blush on both cheeks.
- **Breading:** golden family per `00-style-guide.md`, on the same ramp as
  Nuggy. Pink is the accent only: bows, blush, tongue.
