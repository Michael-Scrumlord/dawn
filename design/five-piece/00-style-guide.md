# Nugget Crew — Style Guide

Master reference: `reference/nuggy-canon.webp` (Nuggy). Every other nugget is drawn to match it.

## What the style is (read off Nuggy)

- **Body:** one plump, golden-orange breaded nugget with a bumpy, crispy texture, dark speckles and tiny crumb dots.
- **Outline:** thick dark-brown/black line, even weight, cel-shaded anime/manga inking.
- **Lighting:** highlight on the upper left, deeper amber shade on the lower right, hard cel edges instead of soft gradients.
- **Limbs:** short, stubby, noodle-like arms and legs in the same golden breading. Rounded mitten hands, rounded feet, no fingers.
- **Face:** large anime eyes with amber-brown irises and white catch-lights. Thick angular eyebrows. Open mouth with a pink tongue when shouting. Sweat droplets for effort.
- **Framing:** full body, three-quarter view, action pose, plain white or transparent background, no ground shadow.

## Consistency rules

**Never changes:** outline weight, eye style, noodle limbs, breading texture, cel shading, background, camera angle.

**Can change:** body proportions, breading tone (stay inside the golden family), accent color, one prop, expression, pose.

**One accent color plus one prop per nugget.** That is what lets the crew read at thumbnail size.

## Negative prompt

> photorealistic, 3D render, real food photo, background scenery, text, watermark, human hands with fingers, extra limbs, different art style, soft airbrushed shading, thin outline

## Workflow

1. Attach `reference/nuggy-canon.webp` to every generation.
2. Do one nugget at a time. Once one is approved, attach it too when generating the next, so the crew drifts toward each other and not away from Nuggy.
3. Generate on plain white, then remove the background afterwards.
4. Work square (2048 x 2048), then upscale if needed.
5. When all five are approved: lineup shot, then an expression sheet per nugget (5 faces each).
