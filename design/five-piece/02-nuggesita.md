## Personality

Nuggesita follows Nuggy into every fight by choice and usually from a different angle. Takes orders from nobody, gives plenty, and is right often enough that it's annoying. Where Nuggy shouts and charges, Nuggesita is still, arms crossed, and already three moves ahead.

## Design notes

- **Contrast with Nuggy:** Nuggy is open and forward. Nuggesita is closed and sideways: arms crossed, body turned slightly away, eyes cutting back at the viewer.
- **Face:** one eyebrow arched, the other pulled low. Crooked, confident smirk. No sweat drops (Nuggy owns those).
- **Bandana:** hot magenta, tied around the top of the body like a headband, knot off to one side.
- **The chip:** a tiny potato chip perched on one shoulder. This is the "chip on the shoulder" gag. Keep it small so it reads as a joke and doesn't become a second silhouette.
- **Body:** slightly leaner than Nuggy, tilted a few degrees, deeper orange breading, same specks and outline.
