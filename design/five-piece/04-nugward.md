## Personality

The crew's calm center. Nugward runs the numbers while everyone else runs at the problem, and he says the odds out loud in a flat, unbothered voice. When Nuggy is at full volume and Nuggesita is halfway out the door, Nugward's steadiness brings them back. He keeps the crew level.

## Design notes

- **Contrast with Nuggy:** Nuggy leans and charges. Nugward stands dead upright, weight balanced, close to symmetrical. The pose should look perfectly level.
- **Glasses:** round, with a cyan tint and white glare glints. They are also the accent color.
- **Face:** half-lidded, relaxed eyes. Small, soft, closed smile. No sweat drops.
- **Holo-tablet:** floats in one hand, showing pi, a sigma and a tiny bar chart in cyan. Keep it small.
- **Bubble level:** a small spirit level clipped to the hip with the bubble centered. It's a visual pun on "keeps the crew level", so keep it subtle.
- **Limbs:** slightly slimmer noodle arms than Nuggy, same outline weight.
