## Name

**Nugsage.** Sage is the herb, the gentle wisdom, and the green accent color, and it's a common unisex name.

Alternates if it doesn't feel right: **Nug-Fern**, **Sproutnug**, **Peanug**.

## Personality

Soft-spoken, with convictions that never move. Nugsage patches up the crew, feeds the crew, and reminds the crew why they're fighting. Nonviolent by choice and never sanctimonious about it. Ask them to join a brawl and they'll show up with bandages and snacks, and somehow that helps more. Will stand between a friend and a dipping sauce.

## Design notes

- **Contrast with Nuggy:** Nuggy raises a fist. Nugsage holds out an open hand. The welcoming gesture is the pose.
- **How "vegan" reads visually:** paler, honey-tan breading, small green herb flecks mixed into the specks, and a two-leaf sprout on top in sage green. It stays playful and never preachy.
- **Face:** the biggest, brightest eyes in the crew, with extra catch-lights. Gentle, open smile. No sweat drops.
- **Satchel:** a small cross-body herb satchel with a leaf-shaped medic patch in sage green.
- **Optional pin:** a tiny enamel pin on the strap in nonbinary-flag colors (yellow, white, purple, black). It's subtle representation, and it can be cut if you'd rather keep the design cleaner.
