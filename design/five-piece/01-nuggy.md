## Personality

Charges first and explains later. Nuggy is the sound the crew rallies around: loud, sweaty, and completely committed. The sweat is effort, never fear. Leads from the front and expects the rest of the crew to keep up or catch up.

## What the canon art shows

- Mid-sprint, leaning forward, one fist clenched and raised.
- Mouth wide open in a battle cry, pink tongue showing.
- Thick, furrowed angular brows, amber eyes locked forward.
- Sweat droplets at the temple.
- Classic golden-orange breading, dark specks, thick dark outline, noodle limbs.

Everything else in the crew is measured against this image.

## Prompts for new Nuggy poses

Attach `reference/nuggy-canon.webp`. Use the master style block from `00-style-guide.md`, then add one of these.

**Team-call pose**
> Nuggy, the exact character in the reference, standing tall with one fist thrust into the air, mouth open in a rallying shout, looking back over the shoulder as if calling the crew forward. Same face, same breading, same outline.

**Victory yell**
> Nuggy, the exact character in the reference, both noodle arms overhead, eyes squeezed shut, mouth wide open in a triumphant roar, a few sweat droplets flying off.

**Off duty**
> Nuggy, the exact character in the reference, slumped and exhausted, half-lidded eyes, tongue out, one noodle arm dangling. Same style and proportions.

## Optional for group art only

A red armband on one noodle arm, so the accent color appears in lineup shots. Skip it on solo art.
